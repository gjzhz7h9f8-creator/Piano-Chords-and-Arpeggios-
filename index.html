Chord Sight-Reading Trainer

Generate musically valid chords from the active key, read them on the staff, then play them on a MIDI keyboard.

Difficulty presets configure the vocabulary automatically, while the other controls let you customise shell shapes, chord quality, generation variety, key-aware special chords, MIDI octave tolerance and recognition mode. Voice-leading generation favours small note movements between successive chords. Wrong MIDI notes are highlighted red, and completed chords require a full release before the next exercise. Power chord + octave exercises use the classic three-note voicing (root–5th–octave), e.g. C–G–C. Practise it as a solid chord or as a broken sequence. The 3-note shell voicing option uses the diatonic 7th chord tones root–3rd–7th in every key. The 2-note power chord and octave options are also available separately.

Training mode

Practice is unlimited. Test ends after 20 completed chords. Timed gives you 60 seconds. Streak mode resets the run after a mistake.
